Prediction markets, built with conviction

Dipiri ran from November 2025 through April 2026. We are shutting it down for now to focus on other things.

Shutdown Note

We built a sharper way to trade prediction markets. Now we are stepping away, for the time being.

Dipiri was built to help people make better bets on Polymarket and other prediction sites by combining market discovery, realtime updates, and integrated trading in one workflow.

In that short run, the product helped contribute ~$2M in revenue to Polymarket through our affiliate relationship, and it helped users spot markets that looked strong, moved fast, and could produce good upside.

We are proud of what we shipped, grateful to the people who used it, and especially interested in what we learned about how traders want to work when markets are moving in realtime.

What we learned

The workflow was the product

01

Integrated trading mattered

The biggest unlock was putting execution next to conviction. Users moved faster when a good market was one click away from action.

02

Realtime context beat static dashboards

Live price movement, flow, and market updates made the product feel useful in the moment instead of interesting in hindsight.

03

Curated screens created edge

The best experience was surfacing markets that looked ready to move, not just listing everything that already existed.
